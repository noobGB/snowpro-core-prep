## Score interpretation

- Pass mark is 750/1000 (roughly 75% correct, though Snowflake uses scaled scoring so it's not
  a strict linear percentage).
- Practice exam #1 sets your baseline — treat anything under ~70% as "this domain needs a full
  re-study day," not just a quick review pass.
- Practice exam #2 should show clear improvement on whatever was weakest in #1. If a domain hasn't
  moved, that's the signal to spend exam-eve time there instead of general review.
